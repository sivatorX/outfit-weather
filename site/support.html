Common questions

ChatGPT answered without the card

ChatGPT sometimes uses web search instead of the plugin. Make sure Outfit Weather is turned on in the chat (the + button under the message box) and start your question with Use Outfit Weather:.

The first answer is slow

Our server sleeps when nobody has used it for a while. The first request can take up to a minute; after that, answers are fast.

"I could not find a city"

Check the spelling or add the country, for example Paris, France or Osh, Kyrgyzstan.

"The day is already over"

We look at the day from 07:00 to 21:00 local time. In the late evening, ask about tomorrow.

How accurate is it?

We average three weather models (ECMWF, ICON and GFS) through Open-Meteo, which is usually closer to the truth than any single model. Weather can still change, so check again in the morning.
