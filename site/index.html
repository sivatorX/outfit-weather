Know what to wear before you step outside

Outfit Weather is a ChatGPT plugin. Ask what to wear in any city today or tomorrow, and it looks at the whole day, from the cold morning to the evening, not just one number.

Add to ChatGPT

How it works

1. Ask

"What should I wear tomorrow in Bishkek?" Just a city, no location access needed.

2. Whole day

Temperature, feels-like, rain, snow and wind from 07:00 to 21:00, averaged from ECMWF, ICON and GFS models.

3. Outfit

A short answer and a card: top, bottom, shoes, what to take, and warnings like "rain from 16:00".

Add it to ChatGPT

  1. In ChatGPT open Settings → Plugins, press +, then Add custom MCP server.
  2. Connection: Server URL, address {{MCP_URL}}
  3. Authentication: No authentication, then Create.
  4. In a new chat turn on Outfit Weather and ask what to wear.

Free and private

No account, no ads, no tracking. The plugin only receives the city name and the day you ask about, and uses them to get the forecast. Read the privacy policy.
