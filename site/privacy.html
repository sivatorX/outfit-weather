Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what data the Outfit Weather plugin for ChatGPT ("Outfit Weather", "we") receives and what we do with it. The short version: we only get the city name and the day you ask about, we use them to get a weather forecast, and we do not store them.

What we receive

When ChatGPT uses Outfit Weather, it sends our server:

  • the city name you asked about (for example "Bishkek"),
  • the day: "today" or "tomorrow".

We do not receive your name, email, account details, chat history or your precise location. We never ask for GPS coordinates.

How we use it

  • We send the city name to the Open-Meteo geocoding service to find its coordinates.
  • We send those coordinates to the Open-Meteo forecast service to get the weather.
  • We turn the forecast into outfit advice and send it back to ChatGPT.

That is the only purpose. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

What we store

  • We do not store the cities you ask about or the answers.
  • Our hosting provider (Render) keeps short technical logs for a limited time: the time of a request, the page address (such as /mcp) and the name of the app that made it. These logs do not contain the city name. We use them only to keep the service working.
  • We use no cookies, no analytics and no tracking on the plugin or this website.

Who else sees data

  • Open-Meteo receives the city name and coordinates to answer weather requests (see the Open-Meteo terms).
  • Render hosts our server.
  • OpenAI runs ChatGPT. Your conversation with ChatGPT is covered by OpenAI's privacy policy, not this one.

We do not sell or share data with anyone else.

Children

Outfit Weather is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal data from them.

Changes

If we change what data we receive or how we use it, we will update this page and the date at the top.
